# dlford.io Redesign 2025 and Post-Mortem

> **Source:** [dlford.io](https://www.dlford.io/dlfordio-redesign-2025/)  
> **Date:** November 21, 2025  
> **Tag:** `homelab`

---

<p></p>&#xA;<img&#xA; alt=&#34;A screenshot of the homepage of this site (dlford.io)&#34;&#xA; &#xA; &#xA; width=&#34;600&#34;&#xA; height=&#34;400&#34;&#xA; src=&#34;https://www.dlford.io/media/rss/dlfordio-redesign-2025-featured.36fea7a290efa4412ca108734ccf995d2a80751ae7fbf0e598613258d5369101_hu_741c03eca3fe76eb.png&#34;&#xA;/>&#xA;<p></p><h2 id=&#34;remember-me&#34;>Remember Me?</h2>&#xA;<p>Hey folks, it&rsquo;s been a while, but I&rsquo;m back with a brand new site and ready to get back to writing! Today, I want to talk openly about what happened and what I&rsquo;m doing to improve.</p>&#xA;<p>In 2022 I rebuilt this site, leaving behind a bloated Gatsby build for what felt like a fresh and clean Hugo and Webpack system. I wrote five articles in 2022 and another one in 2023, I started two new series that I was pretty excited about, I ran a content poll to inform future topics, and then… Silence. For those of you that were waiting for more content I do apologize, this was a pretty clear failure on my part.</p>&#xA;<h2 id=&#34;what-went-wrong&#34;>What Went Wrong?</h2>&#xA;<p>It&rsquo;s hard to really nail down exactly what went wrong, it was certainly a combination of smaller factors that accumulated to this result. I believe these are the top three contenders.</p>&#xA;<h3 id=&#34;focus-and-purpose&#34;>Focus and Purpose</h3>&#xA;<p>Working full time as a software engineer has been very rewarding, and I think the collaborative growth I&rsquo;ve been a part of at work has been satisfying my desire for social learning and knowledge sharing.</p>&#xA;<p>I think there is a balance to be found here, topics I can write about publicly can also be of value to colleagues, and I don&rsquo;t have to spend all of my free time and effort on topics that are proprietary.</p>&#xA;<h3 id=&#34;motivation-and-feedback-loops&#34;>Motivation and Feedback Loops</h3>&#xA;<p>A lot of you have been very supportive and grateful for the time and effort I put into my articles, and I am extremely grateful for that! But nothing stings like spending hours on an article only to have unconstructive negative feedback. This is a problem many content creators face though, it just is what it is.</p>&#xA;<p>Conversely, the feedback loops at work are instant, and they happen on video calls where people are generally less honest about negative or constructive feedback because they&rsquo;re looking me in the eye. It&rsquo;s easy to lean in to that more because it feels better, but I&rsquo;m missing out on the comments that actually prove me wrong constructively, or challenge my perspectives, that&rsquo;s the feedback that ultimately makes me better at what I do.</p>&#xA;<h3 id=&#34;friction&#34;>Friction</h3>&#xA;<p>I think this was the biggest factor, or at least the one I can most attribute to the 2022 redesign. Markdown is fantastic, hands down, I love it. But, opening up a code editor to write an article after being in a code editor all day for work? That just feels like work to me. For a while I tried writing drafts in a few different places and then copying them over so I could jot down ideas as they came to me or make a little progress on my phone while I was waiting somewhere, but this just added friction.</p>&#xA;<p>Friction is a funny concept, how something so minutely inconvenient can so easily zap your motivation to do a task. It feels lazy to admit, but we all have been dissuaded by friction, and sometimes it&rsquo;s hard to recognize the pattern when it&rsquo;s happening to you.</p>&#xA;<h2 id=&#34;actionable-goals&#34;>Actionable Goals</h2>&#xA;<p>Now that we know what went wrong, we need a plan to get back on track.</p>&#xA;<h3 id=&#34;redesign-2025&#34;>Redesign 2025</h3>&#xA;<p>As you can see, I have a brand new site already, the third iteration of my Hugo and Webpack stack, which I intend to write about in detail for the <a&#xA; href=&#34;https://www.dlford.io/blog/series/hugo-and-webpack/&#34;>Hugo and Webpack series</a>&#xA;. Most importantly, I&rsquo;ve integrated <a&#xA; href=&#34;https://decapcms.org/&#34; target=&#34;_blank&#34; rel=&#34;noopener noreferrer&#34;>Decap CMS</a>&#xA; to remove the friction of writing, and I&rsquo;m leveraging render hooks to reduce the use of shortcodes in my content. I&rsquo;ve also changed the theme quite a bit, and trimmed down on some of the fancier features of the site to keep things simple, improved accessibility, and made the navigation much easier to intuit.</p>&#xA;<h3 id=&#34;planned-content&#34;>Planned Content</h3>&#xA;<p>Armed with the results of the content poll I ran before, I am working on a content schedule that I can leverage to take more friction out of writing; what to write about. I&rsquo;ll have a top-down list populated so I can just start writing about whatever is next on the list without making any decisions.</p>&#xA;<p>I&rsquo;ve never had a strict publishing schedule, and I don&rsquo;t intent to change that, but I will be holding myself accountable to dedicate at least one hour a week to writing and/or content planning.</p>&#xA;<p>I&rsquo;m also considering rebooting the <a&#xA; href=&#34;https://www.dlford.io/blog/series/web-authentication/&#34;>Web Authentication series</a>&#xA; from scratch to have things current, and toying with the idea of doing a sort of &ldquo;Season 2&rdquo; of the <a&#xA; href=&#34;https://www.dlford.io/blog/series/how-to-home-lab/&#34;>How to Home Lab series</a>&#xA;, something new folks can jump right into and those that went through all of the existing articles can continue on with as well. I would love to hear your thoughts!</p>&#xA;<h2 id=&#34;other-notable-changes&#34;>Other Notable Changes</h2>&#xA;<p>I am pleased to announce my new full-featured <a&#xA; href=&#34;https://www.dlford.io/rss&#34;>RSS feeds</a>&#xA; that embed the entire articles, and to affirm my commitment to human-written content intended for humans by enacting an <a&#xA; href=&#34;https://www.dlford.io/ai-policy&#34;>AI Policy</a>&#xA;! AI can be a useful tool, but I believe strongly that content creation and educational material is still better produced by humans.</p>&#xA;<h2 id=&#34;are-you-still-reading&#34;>Are You Still Reading?</h2>&#xA;<p>Thank you! Please say hello in the comments, tell me what you&rsquo;re looking forward to, or your thoughts on this topic, or anything you want, I want to hear from you!</p>&#xA;
