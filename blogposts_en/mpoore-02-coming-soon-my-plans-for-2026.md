 This is just a very short post where I will set out some of my plans for the coming year. What are your goals for 2026? Feel free to start a discussion in the comments.


Following on from a disruptive 2024, I had hoped that 2025 would be more settled. However I ended up making another change in the first half of the year and it has taken me some time to find my feet again. So now I feel that I can really make the most of my time and plan for a few personal projects. Here&rsquo;s what I&rsquo;ll be doing in 2026 and what I&rsquo;ll be writing about…


## VCF 9 Homelab Upgrade 
 
 
 
 [#](#vcf-9-homelab-upgrade)
 
 


Yes, I still have a homelab. For me as a consultant it&rsquo;s a very valuable resource as my customers don&rsquo;t tend to like me experimenting with their production systems! Sadly my current homelab doesn&rsquo;t meet the minimum supported specification for VCF 9. Plus, with each host only having 8 CPU cores (16 threads), some solutions are a bit out of my reach.
Early in 2026 I will be upgrading some of my hosts with more compute resources. I plan to share plenty of details about hardware choices and deployment experiences etc. as I go.


## VCF Automation 9 
 
 
 
 [#](#vcf-automation-9)
 
 


One of the updates that will be unlocked in my homelab by the VCF 9 update with VMware VCF Automation 9. I&rsquo;ve been working with this product since version 5 was around and I don&rsquo;t plan to stop any time soon! Having it deployed in my homelab will give me much more freedom to learn and share about the new capabilities. As I said above, I have to be careful when it&rsquo;s someone else&rsquo;s environment!


## VMware Kubernetes Service (VKS) 
 
 
 
 [#](#vmware-kubernetes-service-vks)
 
 


Similarly, VKS is something that I&rsquo;ve learned a lot about both as part of my day job and in my homelab. Many of my homelab services run in VKS clusters. Aside from migrating those to VCF 9, I have a few plans that I want to explore.


## Packer Provisioner for Salt 
 
 
 
 [#](#packer-provisioner-for-salt)
 
 


Whilst I don&rsquo;t envisage massive changes for this in the short-term, there are some roadmap items that I want to tick off. I also expect that I will have to keep up-to-date with any changes to the Salt Minion.


## Certification 
 
 
 
 [#](#certification)
 
 


I haven&rsquo;t kept up with my certifications in the last 18 months. I used to help write a number of the VMware certification exams, which helped keep me current. Who knows, I might get involved in that again, or I might just sit a few exams. As well as VMware certifications, I also plan to take my HashiCorp certifications (which have now lapsed) and some Kubernetes exams also.


## Secret Project 
 
 
 
 [#](#secret-project)
 
 


I have a personal project that I&rsquo;ve been working on over the last few months. The technical bit is 99% done, it&rsquo;s just all of the documentation and supporting artefacts that I need to finish. Otherwise I&rsquo;d tell you about it now. I&rsquo;m looking forward to debuting this though.


## Events 
 
 
 
 [#](#events)
 
 


I haven&rsquo;t been to many events over the last 18 months or so. Most recently I did travel to Amsterdam for a HashiCorp User Group (HUG) meetup. I hope to get back there for KubeCon in March as well as to a few more events throughout the year.


## More… 
 
 
 
 [#](#more)
 
 


Plans are always subject to change. I&rsquo;m sure that there will be plenty more that I get involved in throughout 2026.
